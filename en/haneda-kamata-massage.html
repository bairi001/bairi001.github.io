Massage in Kamata for Haneda Airport Travelers

Staying in Kamata the evening before a flight, or relaxing after arriving in Tokyo? Shin Yuu An is one minute from JR Kamata Station East Exit and open until 2:00 AM.

When this traveler guide is useful

  • After landing at Haneda and staying in Kamata.
  • While staying at a Kamata hotel the evening before a flight.
  • After sightseeing or a long day of walking.
  • After dinner or later in the evening.
  • When two people are traveling together and want to request the same start time.

For a morning flight, visit the evening before if that fits your schedule. The salon opens at 11:00 AM, so we are not an early-morning option.

Choose by how much time you have

  • If you have about 30 minutes: Body 30, Foot Reflexology 30, or Aroma Oil 30 (half body).
  • If you have 45–60 minutes: Foot Reflexology 45/60, Body 60, Aroma Oil 60, or a 60-minute combination course.
  • If you have 70–90 minutes: choose a longer combination, Body 90, Aroma Oil 90, or one of our 70–90 minute set courses.
  • If you have around 120 minutes: Aroma Oil 120 or a 120-minute aroma-and-foot combination is available.

Course availability can vary by therapist and time. Send your preferred course and time first; we will confirm what is available.

JR Kamata and Keikyu Kamata are different stations

Our salon is closest to JR Kamata Station East Exit. Trains from Haneda may bring you to Keikyu Kamata Station, which is a different station. If you arrive there, Shin Yuu An is about a 10-minute walk away. From the West Exit, walk toward JR Kamata through the Keikyu Kamata Asuto shopping street, then continue to Kitajima Building next to FamilyMart.

Walking route from Keikyu Kamata

Traveler FAQ

Can I request an appointment after landing at Haneda?
Yes. Same-day requests are welcome when availability allows. Send the request after you know your arrival and travel time.

Can I come before a morning flight?
We open at 11:00 AM. If you have a morning flight, the practical option is to visit the evening before.

Can two people book together?
Yes. Select 2 guests in the booking form. We will confirm therapist availability and the start time in our reply.

How late can I start?
The latest appointment start time is 11:30 PM. An ¥800 late-night fee applies when you actually arrive at or after 11:00 PM.

Do you provide hotel massage or outcall service?
No. All treatments are provided at our Kamata salon.
